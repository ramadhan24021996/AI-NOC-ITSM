# Glossary

- **ERG (Evidence Reasoning Graph):** A graph structure tracking the AI's logical steps and evidence used.
- **HITL (Human-in-the-Loop):** Processes requiring manual operator approval before execution.
- **NATS:** A high-performance open-source messaging system used as the central nervous system.
- **OPA (Open Policy Agent):** A policy engine paradigm used here to enforce security and business rules on AI decisions.
- **OSI (Open Systems Interconnection):** A 7-layer model used by the system to classify the depth and nature of incidents.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** Enhancing LLM prompts with similar historical incidents fetched from a vector database.
